The **eFTI Regulation** (EU 2020/1056) marks a significant milestone in the digitalisation of freight transport across Europe. But what does it actually mean in practice?

## What is eFTI?

eFTI stands for *Electronic Freight Transport Information*. It establishes a legal framework that allows transport operators to share freight information electronically with authorities, replacing paper-based documentation.

## Who Does It Affect?

The regulation impacts three main groups:

- **Transport operators and logistics companies** who need to provide freight information
- **Competent authorities** who need to access and verify this information
- **eFTI platforms and service providers** who build the digital infrastructure

## Key Deadlines

Member States must be ready to accept electronic freight information through compliant eFTI platforms. The implementation timeline involves multiple phases of testing and piloting across the EU.
