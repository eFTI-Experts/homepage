WHO WE ARE

European Premier eFTI Consultancy

eFTI Experts OÜ is an internationally focused consultancy company, established in Estonia, with seasoned experts specialising in eFTI- related projects. We support EU Member States in their eFTI implementation and development, delivering comprehensive digital solutions for freight transport documentation.

Our team brings together decades of combined experience in digital logistics, EU regulations, and project management. We work closely with government authorities, logistics companies, and technology providers to create seamless digital freight documentation systems.

Our Mission

We strive to lead the transition toward sustainable digital logistics by fostering innovative partnerships, leveraging electronic freight transport information (eFTI), and providing cutting-edge regulatory technology (RegTech) consultancy. Our focus is on supporting authorities and enterprises across Northern and Central Europe, while collaborating with partners in global markets.

Our Vision

To be a trailblazer in sustainable logistics, driving environmental responsibility, pioneering technological innovation, and ensuring seamless regulatory compliance.
